## How it works

My Week shows seven days (Monday to Sunday) plus an **Unscheduled** section with three tabs: _past_ for planned days that have gone by, _mine_ for your own open tasks, and _others_ for every other open task you can see. Planning is per person: the same task can sit on different days for different people.

Switch between **List** and **Board** in the toolbar. Trackr saves your choice with your view preferences. The board shows a column for each of the seven days, followed by **Unscheduled**. Scroll horizontally to reach the other columns.

- Set a task's planned day from the **Plan for** control in its detail panel, or add a new task directly inside a day with **Add task**.
- Capacity is measured against each task's `estimate`. Tasks with no estimate count as **60 minutes**; when time has been logged, the logged time is used instead.
- Capacity is fixed at **8 hours per day** and **40 hours per week** (Monday to Friday). The header shows planned hours against the week, and each day shows its own load bar.
- When a day exceeds capacity, its bar turns red.

> [!WARNING]
> **Capacity is a soft limit**
>
> Trackr warns but never blocks. You can always over-commit. The goal is visibility, not enforcement.

## Quick capture

In **List** view, the **Add task** composer inside each day understands a little shorthand: type a title, add `+SCM` to pick a project, `!high` for a priority, `@name` to assign someone and `~2h` or `~30m` for an estimate. The task is planned onto the day whose composer you opened.

In **Board** view, choose **Add task** in a day column to open the task form with that day and yourself already selected.

## Navigation

- Move between weeks with the arrows, or jump to a week with `?week=2026-09-07` in the URL. Any date works; it snaps to the Monday of that week.
- Trackr remembers the last week you looked at, so reopening the page brings you back where you were.
- The iOS app also supports planning tasks onto days, with the same capacity.
